Privacy Policy

This page explains what Morse Trainer may store when users create accounts, sync practice progress, use the leaderboard, or browse tutorial content.

1. Information we may collect

When you register, log in, or use sync features, the site may process your email address, display name, password hash, practice progress, sync history, statistics, and leaderboard-related records.

The service may also record basic technical data such as request time, route path, IP address, browser type, and device information for stability and security purposes.

2. How this information is used

We use these records to support account access, password changes, session management, progress sync, leaderboard scoring, service maintenance, abuse prevention, and product improvement.

3. Local storage and cloud sync

The site may save some settings and training state in your browser, such as language preference and local progress data, so the experience can be restored when you return.

Cloud sync is used to recover your account data and learning progress across devices. The current public API endpoint is https://api.mmccode.com.

4. Data security

Passwords are not intended to be stored in plain text. Reasonable validation and access controls are used where possible, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The site may use browser storage, cookies, and similar technologies to remember preferences, keep sessions working, restore progress, improve reliability, and support security-related checks.

If advertising or analytics services are enabled, those services may also use cookies or device-level identifiers for reporting, fraud prevention, and ad delivery where permitted.

6. Third-party infrastructure and advertising services

The current site uses Cloudflare Pages, Workers, and related infrastructure. The site may also use third-party services for analytics, monitoring, or advertising, including Google AdSense if advertising is enabled later.

Third-party vendors may process limited technical data needed to deliver their services, and their own privacy terms may also apply to that processing.

7. Your choices

You can log out, clear local browser data, reset practice progress, or clear cloud data from within the product where supported. For account or privacy requests, contact 415417421@qq.com.

You may also manage cookies and related storage through your browser settings. If a consent notice is shown for your region, you may use it to review or change advertising-related choices where available.

8. Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you remove it or your browser clears it. Cloud records may remain while the account exists unless you delete them or request removal.

9. Policy updates

This policy may change when product features, hosting, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

What to include when reporting a problem

Page or feature name

Tell us exactly where the issue happened, such as login, profile, a training mode, or a specific tutorial page.

What you expected to happen

Describe the intended result and what the site actually did instead, including any visible error text.

Browser, device, and time

Include your browser, approximate device type, and when the issue happened so it is easier to trace.

Optional screenshot

A screenshot or short screen recording is often the fastest way to explain layout, rendering, or account-state issues.

Privacy and account requests

If you need help with account records, cloud data removal, or other privacy-related questions, please state the request clearly in your email subject line. That makes it easier to route the request correctly.

For account-specific requests, include the relevant account email address and enough context to verify which record needs to be reviewed.

Before you email

If your question is about how to start, what screen to use, why audio is not playing, or how sync should behave, please check the Help Center and FAQ first. Those pages answer the most common public questions directly.

Direct email is still the right path for account-specific, privacy-related, or repeating technical issues that need case-by-case review.

Response expectations

A reply is usually sent within 3 to 5 business days. Requests that involve account review, data handling, or cross-service debugging may take longer depending on complexity.

Sending one clear email with complete context is more helpful than sending multiple short follow-ups without technical details.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to memorize the whole chart first?

No. Most learners progress faster when they hear a small set of characters repeatedly instead of trying to visually memorize everything at once.

Can I use the trainer without creating an account?

Yes. Core practice works in the browser right away, while account sync is there for cross-device progress and recovery.

What is the best first training path?

Start with the study plan, mix listening and typing drills, then use the tutorials when you hit a recognition or speed plateau.

Is this site only for ham radio users?

No. Amateur radio is one use case, but the trainer is also for hobby learners, self-study, and Morse rhythm practice in general.
